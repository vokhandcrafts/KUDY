## Acceptance criteria — walked from the card

1. ✅ **The autotrigger fires only when all §4.8 conditions hold; each condition
   has an isolated negative test.** The check order is the contract of 11
   §5.1.1: session → eligibility (conditions 4+6) → position freshness and
   radius (5) → suspension (2) → player occupancy (3). Isolated negatives in
   `autotrigger.test.ts`: Paused session ignored entirely + fires after Resume
   (§4.8.1, 11 C9); suspended → auto_fired with manual access, no play
   (§4.8.2, flag arranged on state — the setter events belong to G05.01.c);
   busy → queued, auto_fired untouched (§4.8.3, 11 C8); burned attempt ignored
   and primary heard by hand ignored (§4.8.4 — the proof case, 11 C12), an
   additional story in heard does not block; stale fix 30 001 ms / accuracy
   31 m over a 30 m radius / no fix at all (§4.8.5) plus the inclusive
   boundary pass at exactly 30 000 ms and exactly the radius; locked stop
   ignored with no auto_fired entry (§4.8.6, 11 C33) with an accessible
   control that fires.
2. ✅ **`auto_fired` and `heard` are monotonic; a manual replay after played is
   allowed; an interrupted replay keeps `heard` (C7).** The monotonic flow
   test walks suspension → unlock → queue → deferred play → completion and
   asserts every intermediate entry survives; the replay test launches the
   played story again (play_seq +1, no duplicate heard entry, status stays
   `played`); the C7 test interrupts the replay by another manual play —
   `heard` keeps the fact, the replacement is a `StopAudio` command, never a
   completion.
3. ✅ **The queue is one cell: newest other stop wins, displaced → auto_fired,
   never self-displacement; deferred re-check bounds inclusive.** Tests: the
   second trigger takes the cell and the displaced one lands in auto_fired
   (11 C21) with the status computed per §3.1; a repeated dwell of the queued
   stop keeps the first record and displaces nothing; the deferred play after
   `AudioFinished` fires with a fresh play_id; the boundary test plays at
   exactly 30 000 ms / exactly the radius / exactly 2 × radius, the negative
   triple (30 001 ms / radius+1 / 2×radius+1) retires to auto_fired without
   playing (11 C22); a primary heard by hand while queued blocks the deferred
   play and retires the stop (11 §5.3).
4. ✅ **The three not-played outcomes stay distinct.** One test asserts all
   three from identical arrangements: busy → cell filled, auto_fired empty;
   suspended → auto_fired gained, cell empty (the suspension is checked
   before occupancy); inactive session or stale position → state deep-equal
   to the snapshot. A further test pins the suspended branch over the queue
   at `AudioFinished` time.
5. ✅ **`AudioFinished` only on the matching pair and story_id; late callback
   ignored (C10); `play_seq` +1 per PlayStory.** The gate rejects a mismatched
   story_id (no credit, no stop, no queue start), a completion from the
   previous walk (`session-1` against the `session-2` launch — play_id
   restarts per session, so the pair identifies), a never-minted play_id, and
   the stale play_id of a manual replay (the current launch survives);
   `play_seq` grows by one per launch and rides every `PlayStory` command.
6. ✅ **P01: a heard base never credits extended; extended only through
   `UserSelectedStory`; locked never plays and never enters the lists
   (C33, C36–C37).** The same-version unlock emits `SetGeofenceWindow` only —
   no Play, `heard`/`auto_fired` untouched, the additional story appears in
   `missedStories` beside the untouched stop (C36); dwell launches the primary
   never the additional, a heard base is never replayed by automation, the
   explicit button is the only way to the additional story, its completion
   credits `heard` separately and does not change the marker (C37); the
   locked stop refuses manual and automatic plays, mutates nothing, and is
   excluded from `missedStories` (C33); the paid-only stop becomes a normal
   eligible stop after the unlock (ADR §4.4).
7. ✅ **All six permutations of three stops pass (C31).** The permutation test
   walks every order of crane/plain/gate (gate unlocked first) with one dwell
   + completion per stop: each stop plays its primary, all three land in
   `heard` and `auto_fired`, the session stays `Active` after the last number,
   `play_seq` counts exactly one launch per stop.

## Canon notes — decisions recorded (no shared doc edited)

- **`DwellCompleted` carries `radius`** — 09 §6.1 names the event with
  `stop_id` only, but the queue cell keeps `{stop_id, radius, at}` and the
  deferred re-check reads the radius (11 §5.3); the frozen model's
  `DwellCompleted` carries `radius` too, and G05.01.a explicitly deferred the
  payload to this slice. No doc edited.
- **`PlayStory` carries the bundle-relative `path`** derived per ADR §4.1
  (`<locale>/<tier>/audio/<story_id>.m4a`, tier from `storyTierOf`) — the 09
  §6.1 command signature includes `path` and the engine has every input; the
  controller resolves it against the installed bundle root.
- **Manual play lifts `autoplay_suspended`** (`UserSelectedStop`,
  `UserSelectedStory`) — 09 invariant 7 lists the manual Play among the
  clearing human actions; the setter events (pause/focus/moment) stay with
  G05.01.c, criterion 4 there owns the exclusivity test.
- **Session Pause/End do not stop the guide launch in this slice** — the stop
  is G05.01.c criterion 5 («Session Pause and End stop only guide audio»);
  G05.01.a had nothing playing, this slice introduces guide launches but
  leaves the Pause/End audio transition to the owner slice.
- **`missedStories`** mirrors the model's `missed()` (unit `story_id`, locked
  excluded, route-stop order); the model calls the name legacy, the reducer
  spelling is declared here once.
