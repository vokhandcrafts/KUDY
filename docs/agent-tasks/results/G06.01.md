## Parent acceptance criteria (issue #62), verified at HEAD

1. **One and several guides render without invented cards** — the city and
   rubric surfaces render the published catalog through the production
   `loadCatalog`/`projectGuides` path (fixtures, ordering, the exactly-once
   projection and the route-only honesty are pinned by
   `services/catalog/catalogService.test.ts` and the render tests in
   `app/navigation.test.tsx`; evidence in G06.01.a §Acceptance criteria).
   Sabotage check at HEAD: breaking `byEditorialOrder`
   (`services/catalog/catalogService.ts`) turns 3 of the 28 service-suite
   tests red (the ordering rules) — the protection is live.
2. **Rubric and tariff are not mixed** — the tariff is the canon badge on the
   shared `CatalogGuideCard` (free/paid/mixed, contract values verbatim), the
   rubric renders only with published content (NAV2), NAV3 empty city honest;
   `app/guide-card.test.tsx` + `app/navigation.test.tsx` (evidence in
   G06.01.a §Acceptance criteria 2).
3. **Language, recommended time, open/locked stops are visible** — the
   text/audio languages line is one shared `LocalesLine` on the card and the
   preview (L01 holds), the recommended time comes from the offer's
   `estimated_duration` range or the route `duration_min`, and stop rows show
   the lock badge on locked rows only (evidence in G06.01.a §Acceptance
   criteria 3 and G06.01.b §Acceptance criteria 1;
   `controllers/catalog/previewController.test.ts`, `app/preview.test.tsx`).
4. **The Download/Start button has one meaning** — `derivePreviewButton`
   (`controllers/catalog/previewController.ts`) is the only writer: the real
   inventory state decides Download vs Start, the verify verdict enables it,
   the paid preview without an entitlement never starts. Sabotage check at
   HEAD: replacing the derivation with an always-Start table turns 11 of the
   18 controller tests red (the meaning table, the flip proof, NAV8/NAV9
   guards — the same count G06.01.b recorded), restored, 18/18 green again.

The canonical chain itself is walked in the render tests: City → Guides →
