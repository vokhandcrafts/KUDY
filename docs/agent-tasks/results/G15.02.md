2. **A collection has its own duration assessment rather than a sum of card times** — suite test `criterion 2`: collection offer duration `{75, 105, author_estimate}` deep-equal pinned and strictly greater than the member-card sum `[60, 90]` (guide [45,60] + monastery [15,30]); the editorial log records the arithmetic (transitions + pause reserve, 20 §4.3) and that the collection's distance is deliberately not assessed.
3. **Same place can validly receive an autumn recommendation with a reason and unchanged points** — suite test `criterion 3`: the monastery offer carries exactly one `{season: autumn, reason}` (be/en, ≤280 chars, author's aesthetic judgment per 20 §5); the route's stop `(place_id, position)` sequence deep-equals the author's scenario (no stops added, reordered or re-geolocated), and no other offer gained a season (`[]` stays `not_assessed`).
4. **Unverified opening-hours/access claims are not invented** — suite test `criterion 4` scans every localized card string (offers + projections) against claim patterns (clock times, "opening hours", Belarusian "час працы + digits", free-admission phrases, digit-adjacent prices): zero matches; the monastery card states the unverified state outright («час працы … не правераны; карта не абяцае ні гадзін, ні цаны ўваходу»). Facts are anchored to 1911-dated claims; "today's" status is never asserted.
5. **A collection including a paid guide grants no access and creates no new product** — suite test `criterion 5`: the collection's members include the paid pilot guide (`route.access === 'paid'`), and neither the collection entity nor its offer carries any `product|price|entitlement` key (the closed schema's `additionalProperties: false` restated as a guard); at build time the packager computes collection `access: mixed` from members (G02.03 AC4 suite covers that code path; our package cannot build yet — see limitations) and the runtime cannot Start a Run from it (21 §4). The collection offer's conditions say it outright: «падборка нічога не адкрывае: платнае пашырэнне гіда купляецца асобна».

## Interpretation decisions (documented for review)

1. **No stories in the package, by design.** All pilot drafts are `pending`; `07` reserves `approved` to the human author and `authoring/README.md` reserves the transfer into `content/` to them. The package therefore validates clean but builds only after the transfer (`no-locales` today — recorded, expected). Manufacturing approved-looking stories or an empty-locale ghost guide were both rejected as dishonest.
2. **Pre-assigned story ids.** `route.json` stops reference `story-gdansk-<stop>-{base,ext}` ids that do not exist yet anywhere (validator skips story-ref resolution while no locale layer ships — verified in `validate-package.mjs`, the per-locale loop). Assigning stable ids now implements `09` §3's "generated once, never changed" for `RouteStop`/story identity from birth; the convention is recorded in the editorial log for the transfer task.
3. **Curation avoids duplication (20 §4.2).** The walk stops get no separate place cards and are not collection members; the collection is `[guide, monastery]` — the guide already contains its places. `overlap_note` is correctly absent because no member place equals a guide offer's `suggested_start_place_id` (pinned by test).
4. **Coordinates are approximate by construction** (4 decimals from public map knowledge, 60 m radii) and the editorial log marks them for field verification before publication; the two `radius-overlap` warnings (town hall + Artus Court on one square; St Mary's + the adjacent monastery) are the validator's documented field-check hints, not defects (09 §3 invariant 6) — the suite asserts warnings are only `radius-overlap`.
5. **uk stays out of card texts.** Projections and offers ship be/en; theme labels carry uk (explicitly allowed by 21 §3.2). Ukrainian card texts + native review belong to G14.04.a; authoring them now would create a native-review debt without a task to carry it.
