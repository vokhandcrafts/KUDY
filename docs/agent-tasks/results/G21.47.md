## Acceptance evidence

1. **Dev-client run renders Han text through RN Text; screenshots and per-style machine JSON.** The recorded runs captured 19/19 probe rows at font_scale 1.0 and 2.0 (`han-probe-results.json` `rows[]`: requested style per row, escape-encoded text, bounds and heights at both scales). Every content row paints real glyphs via the system CJK fallback — no tofu on any content row (screenshots `probe-scale1-pos0.png`, `probe-scale2-pos0.png`). Device font inventory (machine, from the run): `/system/fonts` has exactly two CJK files — `NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc`, `NotoSerifCJK-Regular.ttc`; `/system/etc/fonts.xml` declares four CJK families (`zh-Hans`; `zh-Hant,zh-Bopo`; `ja`; `ko`), and each backs the sans slot with `NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc` declared as a **variable font carrying a wght axis 100–900** (ten `<font>` faces per family) plus a `NotoSerifCJK-Regular.ttc` weight-400 fallback. The app's own faces (GolosText 400/600, Alegreya 600, Caveat 400) carry zero Han coverage (G21.40 cmap: 19/67 probe codepoints), so every Han glyph resolves through the system fallback — the render proves the chain end-to-end on the app surface.
2. **Strong-weight resolution: heavier strokes over identical skeletons; the mechanism is not claimed.** There is no separate CJK Bold file on the device (inventory above), but fonts.xml declares the variable wght axis 100–900 on the same `NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc`, so a real variable instance near wght 600 is available to the fallback. The strong row paints visibly heavier strokes over identical glyph skeletons (`strong-weight-compare.png`, regular 400 above, strong 600 below, same string) — consistent with both a variable-font instance and synthetic emboldening, and **this run does not distinguish them**; the machine facts are the file inventory and the declared axis, the mechanism question is a named follow-up for the zh track. The strong style's own request (`GolosText_600SemiBold`, a loaded app face) is unaffected; the observation covers the CJK codepoints it does not cover.
3. **System font_scale 200%: scaling behavior and clipping scan.** Three behaviors on one surface (same string, `glyph-metrics.json` ink heights / `han-probe-results.json` boxes): the app idiom `ScaledText` (cap = font.big-text-factor 1.25) scales ×1.23–1.26; `allowFontScaling=false` stays ×1.0 exactly; a raw uncapped `Text` is **defective** — it paints at ≈1.8× advance with a wrong glyph at the U+53F2 position (the accessibility string stays correct) and ink cut at the accessibility-box bottom. Defect → separate issue [#718](https://github.com/vokhandcrafts/KUDY/issues/718). The clipping scan over the essential rows (definition in the JSON) is **empty at both scales** — every essential element is fully reachable and visible at 1.0 and 2.0 — after the probe's own inset fix (AC5); a second 200% defect outside text scaling, the horizontally fixed actions row overflowing the viewport, went to [#719](https://github.com/vokhandcrafts/KUDY/issues/719).
4. **Regional glyph form: SC, no JP-form trap.** The S2 shared-codepoints row (the ten form-differing codepoints of the G21.40 S2 string, escape-encoded in the surface source) renders at 60 css px on the capped path with **SC forms**: the simplified speech, ice and walk radicals take their simplified shapes, and the bone and straight glyphs match the SC member's designs — matching the G21.40 S2 SC reference (`spikes/G21.40-han-layout/`, S2 section). Screenshot: `probe-scale2-pos2.png` (bottom rows). No JP-form substitution, no TC bleed.
5. **Findings recorded with named follow-ups.** Defects → separate issues (not silent workarounds): [#718](https://github.com/vokhandcrafts/KUDY/issues/718) uncapped RN Text wrong-glyph paint at 200%; [#719](https://github.com/vokhandcrafts/KUDY/issues/719) fixed horizontal action rows overflow the viewport at 200% (production candidates named there: `app/route/[id].tsx`, `app/feedback.tsx`). Probe-surface bring-up lessons (fixed in this task, guarded by `app/han-probe.test.tsx`): scroll insets belong on `contentContainerStyle`, not the frame style — a frame padding never joins the scroll extent and the last rows stayed unreachable behind the gesture bar; without top insets the title climbed under the status bar at 200%. Both are pinned by the insets test (pinned fake insets 50/34 → paddingTop 66, paddingBottom 50).
