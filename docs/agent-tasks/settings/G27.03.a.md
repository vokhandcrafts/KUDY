# G27.03.a — Author two pilot guide briefs for interest tags

## Epic

Epic: #346

## Goal

Give the guide-interest taxonomy two reviewed, source-backed guide briefs before a tag is added to a catalog contract.

## Acceptance criteria

1. [ ] Write briefs for two community angles selected by the owner in G27.01 from actual planned guides, with a named city, intended audience angle, places, claims to verify and source references; mark unpublished content as a proposal. Belarusian and Italian traces are examples, not required choices. If fewer than two angles are approved, record the blocker instead of inventing a second brief.
2. [ ] Editorially check that each angle adds a distinct story rather than merely translating a general guide. Record any unavailable historical evidence and do not publish unsupported claims.
3. [ ] Hand G27.03 candidate tag names and a clear release condition: a tag is visible only when a matching guide is published.

## Out of scope

Publishing or selling guides, changing the catalog schema and inferring a reader's nationality.

## Dependencies

Blocked-by: #781.

## Proof

Two source-backed briefs and an editorial review record; no production availability claim.
