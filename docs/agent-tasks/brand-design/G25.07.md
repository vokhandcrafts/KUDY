## Acceptance criteria

1. [ ] Create linked phone/web mockups for entry/city, guide list, preview/download/start, active walk with transcript/pause/resume/end, My KUDY, language/settings and feedback. Inventory what exists versus deferred states before drawing; preserve navigation, free stop order and explicit Start.
2. [ ] Demonstrate offline/failed download, unavailable content language, locked access, denied GPS and returning from an interruption. Do not let opening a screen, a purchase or a souvenir start narration.
3. [ ] Use the brief scene to show where the dragon appears, stays silent and has a sound placeholder. Collection/achievement and Nearby experiments must have a visible prototype-only status and remain outside the current production scope.
4. [ ] Keep the web entry understandable without installation; show a link-based introduction and optional app continuation. Deliver local HTML/CSS sources, screenshots, interaction map and references to exact glossary/component revisions.

## Out of scope

Deploying a site, changing navigation/audio/purchase contracts, implementing collections or introducing a required install wall.
