## Acceptance criteria

1. [ ] Explore at least six distinct object silhouettes tied to concrete story/experience themes, including the brief’s Reflection example. Compare small list thumbnails and a detailed view; carry common clay/material rules across cities while varying the motif.
2. [ ] Provide source artwork and prototypes for personal souvenir, condition-verified achievement, not-yet-earned and unknown/unverified states. Labels, empty collection and detail copy must distinguish voluntary saving from proven activity.
3. [ ] For each object record narrative meaning, candidate condition, evidence actually available, offline/repeat behavior and open decisions. Never infer a visit, observation or emotion from opening a card; no streak, random points or guilt copy.
4. [ ] Hand candidate mechanics to #695 and #696 for pilot selection/contract review. This task closes on the art kit and explicit unresolved-condition ledger, not on approval or implementation of new progress, analytics or storage.

## Out of scope

Implementing an achievement engine, new schemas/events, proving physical visits, selecting a pilot on behalf of #695, or building physical treasure campaigns.
