## Acceptance criteria

1. [ ] Design an app-store screenshot composition, a web introduction block, a social card and a QR invitation card from the shared palette, typography, dragon kit and vocabulary. Use platform requirements verified at execution time.
2. [ ] Show Belarusian and English examples plus text-expansion rules; use real application capabilities and clearly label concept screenshots. QR prototypes must identify their sample destination and must not promise a gift or available cache.
3. [ ] Deliver editable HTML/SVG sources and raster exports with dimensions, safe areas, contrast, rights and image-source records. Link the selected icon/splash; do not start an independent visual direction.
4. [ ] Keep physical treasure campaigns and compass secrets in the deferred ledger with links to #695; publishing campaigns, placing items and distribution remain separate decisions.

## Out of scope

Publishing to stores/social media, buying ads, placing QR stickers or gifts, and designing the full treasure campaign.
