# G23.09 — Android E2E: purchase, unlock and restore (sandbox)

Issue: [#667](https://github.com/vokhandcrafts/KUDY/issues/667).

## Epic

Epic: #658

## Goal

The purchase part of Journey 2 runs on the emulator with a store sandbox account: buying unlocks the extension in the current session without losing progress, failures keep the free part working, and restore works.

## Acceptance criteria

1. [ ] A locked stop shows only its open teaser; the calm offer is at the bottom of the description, with no modal.
2. [ ] Purchase → download of the same-version extension → stops unlock in the current session, progress is kept, no audio starts by itself.
3. [ ] Payment failure offers Try again and Continue free, also after the third attempt; the free part keeps working.
4. [ ] Paid but download failed: "Purchased · needs download" with retry, never a second purchase.
5. [ ] Restore Purchases in My KUDY restores access on a clean install with the same store account.
6. [ ] Access comes from the server-side check, never from the client alone (asserted through the sandbox grant path, not a mocked flag).

## Out of scope

Real payments, Apple, cross-store transfer.
