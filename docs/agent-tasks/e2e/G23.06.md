# G23.06 — Android E2E: Nearby, discovery, Moments and feedback

Issue: [#664](https://github.com/vokhandcrafts/KUDY/issues/664).

## Epic

Epic: #658

## Goal

Journey 3, "Open a guide nearby" and the discovery and feedback journey (`docs/03_user_journeys.md`; `docs/20_discovery_and_feedback.md` D01–L02) run on the emulator.

## Acceptance criteria

1. [ ] Discovery: city → real offers → time/theme filter → place, collection and guide preview; a paid guide is marked, but tapping it neither buys nor starts it; zero results show honest alternatives.
2. [ ] Nearby with granted location and a geo fix shows the map and the fixture Moment; the Moment does not play by itself; explicit Play plays its teaser.
3. [ ] During an active walk, explicit Moment Play stops the guide and blocks its autoplay until "Continue guide".
4. [ ] A geo fix near a stop of another guide shows the nearby-guide hint; tapping it opens the preview; going back does not change the active session.
5. [ ] Feedback after End: sending while the origin is down queues it; it is sent once the origin is back; edit and delete work; no public rating appears anywhere.

## Out of scope

Moment density calibration (field work), new discovery features.
