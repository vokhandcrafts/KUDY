# G05.04 — Queue and automation blocking across the real boundaries

**Status:** not-started. **Model class:** strong. **Dependencies:** [G05.01.d](G05.01.d.md) (#201), [G05.02.b](G05.02.b.md), [G05.03.a](G05.03.a.md) and [G06.09.b](../app/G06.09.b.md) (the `controllers/` layer and its rules) complete. Backlog row: G05.04 ([16](../../16_delivery_backlog.md)).

## Scope

G05.01.b already implements the one-cell queue and the three outcomes inside `step()`. This task proves them where they can break in practice: when fixes arrive through `services/location` → `acceptFix`, and audio callbacks and focus events arrive through `services/audio`. It adds one framework-free orchestrator that wires the real pipeline, reducer and both services (with fake OS ports and an injected clock) and the scenario tests on it. No new rule is written; a gap found in `step()` is fixed there with its own test.

Read: [09 §6.1](../../architecture/09_technical_architecture.md) (queue paragraph, three outcomes, invariants 7–8), §6.4 (manual tap and GPS trigger share one path); [11](../../11_run_interaction.md) §5.1–§5.4, criteria C5–C12, C18–C22; [19 §4.3, §5.2–§5.3, §6.1–§6.5](../../architecture/19_class_and_module_map.md); results of G05.01.b–.d, G05.02.b, G05.03.a.

## Acceptance

1. [ ] Newest other stop displaces the queue: while stop A plays, B then C trigger through the pipeline; after A finishes, C plays and B is in `auto_fired`; a repeated trigger of A while A plays does not displace itself.
2. [ ] The deferred Play re-checks the **latest accepted** fix: if the last pipeline-accepted fix is older than 30 000 ms or farther than `2 × radius`, the queued stop does not play and goes to `auto_fired`; bounds inclusive, both sides tested.
3. [ ] A fix rejected by the pipeline never refreshes `last_fix` (a spike fix after the person walked away cannot unlock a deferred play).
4. [ ] After `UserPausedAudio`, a call (`FocusLoss` from the audio service) or an explicit Moment play, no automatic start happens until the explicit actions of invariant 7; a trigger in that window goes to `auto_fired` with manual access.
5. [ ] A manual tap on a stop and a GPS trigger enter `step()` through the same orchestrator path (one test drives both and compares the command streams).
6. [ ] A late audio callback from before a Pause/End/new Start is ignored end to end (no heard, no queue start).
