Use case: ui-mockup and brand concept board.
Create ONE exceptionally art-directed, coherent visual development board for KUDY, a Belarusian-language app for thoughtful authored city walks starting in Gdańsk. Landscape 3:2, high resolution. This is a provisional visual proposal, not working UI or final assets.
REFERENCE INPUTS: image 1 a1.png is the existing ceramic dragon silhouette/character reference; image 2 b2.png is the existing simplified ivory-on-green ceramic relief and material reference. Preserve their long raised neck, small angular head, stocky pear-shaped body, great membranous wings, curled tail, tactile hand-worked ceramic. These are reference images, not edit targets. Do not turn dragon into big-eyed children's cartoon, sleek fantasy monster or generic cute mascot. Make him an experienced, gently mischievous, attentive walking companion. Warm clay/brown glaze in character illustrations, ivory relief only in icon study.
ART DIRECTION: contemporary Baltic editorial field notebook with restrained ceramic relief and carefully drawn city fragments. Sophisticated and warm. Warm paper #FAF7F2, deep green #1D6B4F for functional actions, ink #22262B for text. Clay brown and tiny amber glints only illustrative accents. Very faint paper on quiet outer board and discovery illustration, completely flat clean surfaces on map/player and behind small text. No distressed antique parchment, pirate cartography, heavy fake leather or ornament everywhere. Gdańsk expressed through narrow facades, stepped/curved gables, brick-and-stone contrast, carved waterspout fragment and waterfront crane as precise selective drawings. Architectural motifs stay in illustrations, never as decorative navigation or fake historic logos. Warm authorial seriousness, not tourism brochure, travel dashboard or game.
LAYOUT: quiet, beautifully balanced editorial board. At top left small KUDY wordmark, main Belarusian title "Гліна. Папера. Горад." and small subtitle "Эскіз візуальнага стылю".
Leftmost third: one beautifully modeled clay dragon about 22% overall board height, then a small row of 3 restrained same-dragon pose studies (calm, curious, inviting). Below, an app icon study: rounded green square with simplified ivory relief raised dragon head and neck; enough negative space for small size. Alongside, one consistent small row of clean outline functional icons (play, pause, headphones, download, map pin, transcript), these are functional icon studies, no label required. A short narrow color strip and small architectural detail vignette. Leave generous space.
Right two-thirds: three tall straight-on mobile screen compositions side-by-side, realistic large legible text, simple thin device frames, all same size. Screens dominate height.

A carefully drawn two-color Gdańsk waterfront/crane illustration in a broad editorial image area with ample pale space, small clay dragon peeking nearby. SCREEN 3 ACTIVE WALK: mostly clean legible schematic street map with river, simple recommended dotted route, 3 points, current location distinct; no ornamental fantasy map. Make all three screens look like the same usable, thoughtful app, not three posters. Realistic touch targets, strong contrast, very restrained 10px corners, green buttons with subtle bottom shelf. Do not introduce coins, achievements, collectibles, notifications, upsells, city-wide decorative borders, giant gradients or glassmorphism.
