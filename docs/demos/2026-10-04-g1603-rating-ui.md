# #74 — voluntary private rating UI: invitation, form and honest delivery

*Showboat demo for issue #74 (G16.03, `controllers/useFeedbackController` +
the `app/` rating surfaces), created 2026-10-04.*

20 §7 promises the rater a voluntary rating that is never preselected and
never blocks anything, an invitation only after local guide use and at most
once per session, an explicit Send separated from the analytics consent, and
the honest delivery distinction — «захавана на прыладзе» is not «адпраўлена»,
a delete that has not reached the server says so. The demo drives the
production controller over the real G16.02 queue (node:sqlite storage, a
scripted transport, the fixed clock and mutation id), so the trace is
deterministic: the invitation gates, the offline round stays pending, the
acked round lands `sent`, the acked edit re-sends against the acknowledged
revision and the tombstone waits for the network.

Reading the trace: zero heard stories invite nothing; two heard stories offer
the ended session's own pinned identity (`guide-route-a1` v1/be — never a
catalog read); a dismissed session is never offered again. The form opens
with `score=null` — no default star. The offline Send keeps the value
`pending` on the device; the online round lands `sent` at revision 1 under
the pinned disclosure `feedback-disclosure-1`; the edit re-sends against the
acknowledged revision (2); the delete renders the delete-pending state until
its CAS-tombstone is acknowledged (`score=null`, revision 3). The full
acceptance suite is `tests/feedback/ui-controller.test.ts` and the render
suites `app/feedback.test.tsx`, `app/run.test.tsx`, `app/my.test.tsx`,
`app/place/place.test.tsx`.
